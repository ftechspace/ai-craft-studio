# Photo & grade playbook (photo-grader)

- **Choose moments, not frames.** Scan for the peak of an emotion (the split second a laugh breaks, a glance, an
  unguarded gesture), the decisive moment. Offer alternatives the brief didn't ask for if they're better.
- **Crop with intent:** tight crops for emotion, extreme negative space for loneliness/scale, cut-off edges for
  energy. A 2 MP frame cropped tight with a deliberate soft, grainy aesthetic beats a sharp but empty wide.
- **A look is an idea.** Name what the grade *means* (memory, heat, darkroom, morning after) and derive the moves
  from it (faded blacks = memory, split-toned highlights = film stock, local light = attention). Vary the look across
  a story only when the story changes.
- **Light the subject in post:** radial masks to pull attention to faces/hands, gentle dodge on eyes, burn on
  distracting brights (light stands, cables, exit signs).
- Darker skin stays dense, warm and textured; never flatten it to make the frame "bright".

## Generic tells to avoid
Default warm-film preset on everything · centred subjects · wides chosen because they were in the brief · uniform
global grades with no local light.
