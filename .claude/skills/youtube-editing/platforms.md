# Platform Specs

Platforms change limits often. Treat numbers here as defaults and check the platform's
current help pages before a deliverable where a limit matters (e.g. max length).

FilmCraft exports H.264 MP4 with AAC, which all platforms below accept.

## YouTube long-form
- Aspect: 16:9. Resolution: 1920x1080 or 3840x2160. Keep the source frame rate.
- Bitrate: high (YouTube re-encodes; upload better than you think you need).
- Loudness: about -14 LUFS integrated, true peak no higher than -1 dBTP.
- Captions: upload SRT; don't burn in unless it's the style.
- Chapters: in the description, first at 0:00, at least 3 chapters, each at least 10 s.
- Thumbnail: 1280x720 (16:9), under 2 MB; readable at phone size.
- End screen: last 5-20 s.

## YouTube Shorts
- Aspect: 9:16. Resolution: 1080x1920.
- Length: up to 3 minutes (check current limit); most perform best under 60 s.
- Loudness: about -14 LUFS, peak no higher than -1 dBTP.
- Captions: burned in.
- Safe zone: keep text and faces out of the bottom ~20% and the right edge (UI buttons),
  and the top ~10%.

## TikTok
- Aspect: 9:16. Resolution: 1080x1920.
- Length: upload limits are long, but short performs best; aim for 15-60 s unless the
  brief says otherwise.
- Loudness: about -14 LUFS.
- Captions: burned in.
- Safe zone: avoid the bottom ~20-25% (caption and description area) and the right
  edge (buttons).

## Instagram Reels
- Aspect: 9:16. Resolution: 1080x1920.
- Length: up to 3 minutes (check current limit).
- Loudness: about -14 LUFS.
- Captions: burned in.
- Safe zone: similar to TikTok; also check how the 4:5 grid crop of the cover looks.
- Cover image: design it to read when cropped to the centre.

## Multi-platform rule
Make one master edit, then create a version per platform: reframe, re-caption and
re-pace for each. Don't just stretch or letterbox a 16:9 edit into 9:16.
