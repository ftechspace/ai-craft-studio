# Creative review

Technical checks (sync, levels, sizes) are not this. This asks: **would a person stop, feel something, remember
it, and want to share it?** Be honest and specific; politeness that ships mediocre work is a failure.

## How to look
- Images: view at full size **and** at the size people meet them (168×94 thumbnail, phone-width poster, 64 px logo).
- Films: read a contact strip of the whole piece in order (one frame per shot + every overlay moment) and the
  structure/turns list; read the cue sheet and the score build file for musical shape (you can't hear — judge the
  writing: motif, development, dynamics, silences).
- Read the piece's **Creative intent** block. Judge whether the work delivers *that* idea, and whether the idea was
  worth having.

## Score 1–5 (5 = would impress a professional)
| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Hook | Does the first 3 s / first glance grab and make me want more? |
| Idea | Is there one clear, specific idea? Can I say it in a sentence? |
| Surprise | Did anything make me go "oh!"? Could a template have made this? |
| Emotion | Do I feel the intended thing (laugh, tension, warmth, awe)? Where exactly? |
| Specificity | Is it built from real details of this material, or generic stand-ins? |
| Coherence | Do all parts speak one language (type, colour, motion, sound, cut)? |
| Fit | Would this sit naturally next to the user's references (cozy day-in-life, calm Japan walks, MAZELEE family films, couple-shoot BTS)? |
| Craft | Is execution clean enough not to distract? |

**PASS** needs ≥ 4 on Idea, Surprise, Emotion, Hook and Fit, and nothing below 3. Otherwise **REVISE**.

## Generic / AI-tells checklist (each one found costs a point on Surprise)
- Centred title + small tracked subtitle; everything symmetrical; all elements medium size
- HUD/REC/viewfinder brackets, light leaks, glitch, film burns used as default "cinematic"
- Labels or captions that describe what the picture already shows; text on every scene
- Uniform rhythm (all shots ≈ same length; every section same energy); music wall-to-wall at one density
- Four-on-the-floor + saw arpeggio; a 4-chord loop with no motif or development
- Tiny people in wide frames used as thumbnails/posters; photo-top/title-middle/three-pics-bottom posters
- Effects with no motivation (flashes, whooshes, zooms on every cut)
- Safe grade preset on everything; no local light
- Ending that just fades instead of landing a button or callback
- **Against this user's house style (`taste-profile`):** no clear story spine · music wall-to-wall or never switched
  on/off by anything · no natural sound or silence · text describing the picture / labels on every scene · a designed
  text-heavy thumbnail instead of a real emotional frame · synthetic flashes where a real event could do the job ·
  trailer/ad devices (HUD, countdowns, suspense stingers) in intimate real-life material · a big ending instead of a
  small one that rhymes with the opening

## Output format
```
Verdict: PASS | REVISE
Scores: Hook n · Idea n · Surprise n · Emotion n · Specificity n · Coherence n · Fit n · Craft n
Best thing: <the one moment/element that works and must be protected>
Notes (max 5, most important first):
1. <what's wrong, where (time/area)> → <exactly what to do instead>
...
Bolder option: <one braver alternative direction worth trying>
```
Notes must be actionable in our apps ("crop to the two faces from 0021 @8.3, faces 45 % of height; put REC in the
empty left third") — never vague ("make it pop").
